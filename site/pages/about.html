Our story

Built by veterans, for the businesses that need them

Evogr Solutions was born from the vision of military veterans who recognised the untapped potential in India's workforce. After decades of serving the nation, our founders channelled their leadership experience into building a bridge between businesses seeking reliable talent and skilled professionals seeking meaningful opportunities.

Since our inception, we've grown from a small team of dedicated veterans to a nationwide network of over 7,000 professionals, serving clients across 28 states. Our military heritage shapes everything we do, from our rigorous vetting process to our unwavering commitment to mission success.
