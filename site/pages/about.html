About Evogr Solutions

Founded on military principles, driven by excellence, committed to your success.

Built by veterans, for businesses that need people they can rely on.

Evogr Solutions was born from the vision of military veterans who saw untapped potential in India's workforce. After decades of serving the nation, our founders used their leadership experience to build a bridge between businesses that need reliable talent and skilled professionals who want meaningful work.

We have grown from a small team of dedicated veterans into a nationwide network of over 7,000 professionals, serving clients across 28 states. Our military background shapes everything we do, from how we vet people to how we run a deployment.
