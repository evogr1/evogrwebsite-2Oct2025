Safety Guidelines

At Evogr Solutions we are committed to the highest safety standards across all our operations. Every professional in our network is expected to follow these guidelines to keep themselves, their colleagues and clients safe.

Personal safety

  • Always wear appropriate personal protective equipment (PPE)
  • Follow all safety protocols and procedures at work sites
  • Report any unsafe conditions immediately to supervisors
  • Attend mandatory safety training sessions

Emergency procedures

  • Know the location of emergency exits and assembly points
  • Understand fire safety procedures and evacuation routes
  • Keep emergency contact numbers readily available
  • Report incidents and near-misses promptly

Work site standards

  • Maintain a clean and organized work environment
  • Follow client-specific safety requirements
  • Use equipment only as trained and authorized
  • Ensure proper lifting techniques to prevent injury

Team safety

  • Look out for colleagues and promote safety awareness
  • Communicate hazards and safety concerns effectively
  • Support new team members with safety orientation
  • Participate actively in safety meetings and drills

Emergency contacts

  • Emergency services: 112
  • Evogr support: +91 9045370080
  • Email: safety@evogr.net

Safety resources

  • Safety training materials available on request
  • Regular safety updates and bulletins
  • 24/7 support for safety-related concerns

Questions or concerns?

If you have any questions about these safety guidelines or need to report a safety concern, please contact our safety team.
