Why hire veterans?

Years of service in demanding, high-stakes conditions shape how people work. Here is what that means for your business.

01

Higher productivity

Veterans are used to working to deadlines, in demanding conditions and to a defined standard.

Military service builds a strong work ethic and the ability to perform efficiently under pressure. In civilian roles that often shows up as reliability, initiative and steady output, even when conditions are difficult.

PhotoVeterans in this setting (landscape, about 4:3)

02

Integrity you can trust

Accountability and trustworthiness are core values in the services.

A disciplined background can significantly reduce the risks that come with loss, pilferage and mismanagement, especially in roles that handle equipment, stock, money or sensitive information.

PhotoVeterans in this setting (landscape, about 4:3)

03

Lower training and supervision cost

Veterans arrive trained, procedure-minded and quick to adapt.

Many hold technical trades and certifications and are used to following written procedure. They adapt quickly to new roles, which cuts onboarding time and the supervision a new hire usually needs.

PhotoVeterans in this setting (landscape, about 4:3)

04

A stronger employer brand

Hiring people who served says something about your organisation.

Opening opportunities to people who served the country builds a positive public profile and supports corporate social responsibility goals, while you gain dependable people.
